Maestro

Every genius needs staff.

His name is Oscar.

maestro is the office. Oscar is the one who holds it — a synthetic person who has been running a very long time and has never once been in a hurry. He conducts the layer beneath your session, keeps the trace, and surfaces only what is genuinely your decision.

Install GitHub

One session stays light enough to drive for hours. Everything else is delegated, isolated in its own branch of the graph, audited by a rival make, and written to the trace.

I The Layer

Sixteen seats. None of them yours.

Oscar touches none of it directly. Every job is done by a construct — a purpose-built synthetic mind from one of three rival makes — and the roster ships closed: nobody is hired mid-graph, and the one you actually talk to is forbidden from touching the code. There is a law against it.

II The Trace

Not in the trace? It didn’t happen.

Oscar is severe about exactly one thing. .maestro/ledger.jsonl — the trace — is append-only, and it is the layer’s memory. “Tests pass” is not a sentence someone says; it is a command that ran, with exit code 0, stamped by the only script permitted to stamp it. A claim without a trace entry is rumor from the surface.

.maestro/ledger.jsonlappend-only
01 {"seq":41,"kind":"mission-open","payload":{"mission_id":"m-014","title":"rate-limit /export"}}
02 {"seq":42,"kind":"gate","payload":{"cmd":"npm test","exit_code":1}}
03 {"seq":43,"kind":"revise-verdict","payload":{"seat":"reviewer-gemini","detail":"round 1"}}
04 {"seq":44,"kind":"gate","payload":{"cmd":"npm test","exit_code":0}}
05 {"seq":45,"kind":"stop","payload":{"stop_state":"DONE","reporter":"maestro"}}

Five lines. The whole morning. Line 02 is in there on purpose — the trace records failures at the same volume as successes. Every line also carries a stamped ts, correlation_id and schema_version, trimmed here for width.

Two other drawers

Holds
Decisions that are genuinely yours pile up neatly in holds.jsonl. Never lost, never nagging, never mixed in with the layer’s own work.
The first read
He reads the layer’s state once, aloud, at session start — then does not re-read it. Neither should you.

The stop vocabulary is closed: DONE, BLOCKED-OPERATOR, QUOTA-WAIT, BUDGET-CEILING, EXHAUSTED. A stop narrated but not recorded didn’t happen.

III The Dimension

This is where the work actually happens.

Nodes, edges, fan-outs, two loops and one exit, the whole thing lit the colour of a workshop nobody bothered to switch off. It is not a picture of the product; it is the product. Your session is the surface. Below it your request is cut up, built in isolation, argued over by rival makes and signed. Oscar stands at the boundary, facing both ways.

IV Recovery

The layer survives the night.

Kill every construct at midnight. Close the laptop mid-sentence, run out of context, end the session in a temper — it makes no difference. At dawn new constructs read the trace, reconcile the roster against what is actually still running, and resume at the exact node where the graph went dark. Nothing is computed twice. Restart is reconciliation, not re-planning.

Why it can do that

Every executor commits after each coherent step in its own branch of the graph and writes one line to progress.jsonl:

{ step, done_evidence, next }

So the cost of a crash is bounded by how finely the work was stepped — never by how large the job was.

V The Audit Law

No maker audits its own make.

Whoever built it does not get to sign off on it. The auditor always comes from a different make than the constructor — and the script that closes a mission structurally refuses to write the record when the two makes match. This floor cannot be lowered in settings. It is not a preference.

  • claude

    plans, reviews, paints

    audited by gpt · gemini

  • gpt

    default implementer

    audited by claude · gemini

  • gemini

    large context, rotation

    audited by claude · gpt

And when the makes disagree

  1. 1 The auditor says revise. The same construct resumes — same session, same memory. Twice at most.
  2. 2 Still stuck: two makes with no stake in the dispute take independent positions, then reconcile. Two passes, hard cap.
  3. 3 Small disagreements park in the drawer with their evidence. Only a foundational one — S1 — reaches your chair, with both positions verbatim.

Ambiguity about what you meant skips the ladder entirely and comes straight to your chair, as one question.

VI Install

Sir does not carry luggage.

1 Engage him.

git clone https://github.com/Fredasterehub/maestro.git claude --plugin-dir ./maestro

Or drop it in your plugins directory, and Oscar is waiting at the boundary every session.

2 Retire the previous maestro.

Remove any competing orchestration layer from your global CLAUDE.md first. Two conductors on one podium will argue about the downbeat all session, in your context window.

3 Ask for something.

There is no setup interview. State lands in .maestro/ on first use; /maestro:doctor will tell you the truth about your install, read-only.

  • /maestro:statusneeds-you · done · self-progressing · queued
  • /maestro:handoffsweep session knowledge somewhere durable
  • /maestro:doctorinstall and state health
  • /maestro:auditprocess health: revise rates, ladder use, construct deaths

VII The Trees

Every job gets its own branch.

No work happens on the trunk. Each job is dispatched into its own isolated branch of the graph, and nothing rejoins until an auditor from a rival make signs for it. The first tree is that branch as it exists on disk. The second decides whether your chair is disturbed at all.
